## Guttering, fascias and soffits

Your roofline does an important job: it carries rainwater away from the walls and protects the roof timbers. When gutters overflow or fascias rot, water runs down the brickwork and can cause damp inside.

### Typical roofline costs (2026)

| Job | Typical cost |
| --- | --- |
| Full uPVC fascias, soffits and gutters, 3-bed semi | £1,800 – £3,500 |
| Full uPVC fascias, soffits and gutters, detached | £3,000 – £6,000 |
| uPVC guttering only, per metre | £25 – £45 |
| Aluminium or cast iron guttering, per metre | £60 – £120 |

Scaffolding is the biggest variable and is sometimes included in the price. Always check.

### Signs your roofline needs work

- Gutters overflowing, sagging or dripping at the joints
- Rotten, flaking or split timber fascias
- Damp patches or green staining on the outside walls
- Birds or wasps getting in behind the soffits

## Chimney repairs

Many Birmingham homes still have their original chimney stacks, even where the fireplaces are long gone. Exposed to weather on all sides, they need attention from time to time.

### Typical chimney costs (2026)

| Job | Typical cost |
| --- | --- |
| Repoint a chimney stack | £400 – £1,200 |
| Patch repair to lead flashing | £150 – £500 |
| Replace lead flashing around the stack | £600 – £1,500 |
| Rebuild the stack above the roofline | £1,500 – £3,500 |
| Scaffolding or access for chimney work | £400 – £700+ |

Prices are typical 2026 UK ranges and vary with height and access. A good roofer will tell you if a cheaper repair, such as repointing or new flashing, will solve the problem before suggesting a rebuild.

### Signs your chimney needs attention

- Crumbling or missing mortar between the bricks
- Damp patches on the chimney breast inside
- Cracked or lifting lead where the chimney meets the roof
- A leaning stack, or bits of brick and mortar in the gutter
