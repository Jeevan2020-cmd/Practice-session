Flat roofs are everywhere in Birmingham: on rear extensions, garages, dormers, bay windows and kitchen extensions. They're also the roofs most likely to leak, usually because of age, ponding water or a tired felt covering.

## Flat roof materials compared

| Material | Typical cost per m² | Typical lifespan | Good for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Felt (torch-on, multi-layer) | £60 – £100 | 10 – 20 years | Tight budgets, simple garage roofs |
| EPDM rubber | £80 – £120 | 20 – 30 years | Extensions and garages; one-piece membrane with few joins |
| GRP fibreglass | £100 – £160 | 25 – 40 years | Neat finish, roofs you'll walk on, awkward shapes |

Prices are for supply and fit, from 2026 UK price guides. A warm-roof upgrade with insulation can add roughly £30 to £60 per m², and replacing a rotten deck adds roughly £15 to £35 per m².

## Signs your flat roof needs attention

- Puddles that sit on the roof for days after rain
- Cracks, blisters or bubbles in the felt
- Soft or spongy patches when pressed, which can mean the deck is rotting
- Damp patches on the ceiling below
- Moss and plant growth along the edges

## What to ask for in a flat roof quote

- Which material and system, and the manufacturer's guarantee
- Whether the old deck is being replaced or just covered over
- Whether insulation is being added (a "warm roof")
- How the edges, gutters and wall upstands will be finished
- The workmanship guarantee in writing
