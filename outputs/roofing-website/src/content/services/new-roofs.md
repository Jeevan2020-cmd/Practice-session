## New roof prices in Birmingham (2026)

| Property and material | Typical cost |
| --- | --- |
| Mid-terrace, 2 to 3 bed, concrete tiles | £4,500 – £8,000 |
| Semi-detached, 3 bed, concrete tiles | £6,000 – £11,500 |
| Semi-detached, 3 bed, clay tiles | £9,500 – £15,500 |
| Semi-detached, 3 bed, natural slate | £11,000 – £18,500 |

Ranges are from 2026 UK price guides and generally include scaffolding, stripping the old roof, new battens and a breathable membrane. Some quotes leave out scaffolding or skip hire, so check what's included before comparing prices. Detached houses and complex roofs with dormers, valleys or hips cost more.

## What a proper re-roof includes

A full replacement is more than new tiles. A good quote should list:

1. **Scaffolding** for safe access, and a skip for the old materials
2. **Stripping** the old tiles, battens and felt back to the rafters
3. **Checking the timbers** and replacing any rotten rafters or boarding (often priced separately once it's opened up)
4. **New breathable membrane** and **treated battens**
5. **New tiles or slates**, fixed to current standards
6. **Ridge and hips**, ideally with a dry-fix system that doesn't rely on mortar
7. **Lead flashing** around chimneys and walls
8. **Insulation upgrade** where building regulations require it
9. **A written guarantee** on the workmanship

If a quote is a single line like "new roof: £5,000", ask for it to be itemised.

## When is it time for a new roof?

Replacement usually makes more sense than repair when you see:

- Repeated leaks in different places, year after year
- Tiles that are crumbling, flaking or sliding
- A sagging roof line, which can mean the battens or timbers have failed
- Torn or brittle felt in the loft, or none at all on older homes
- A roof that's simply reached the end of its life

## Planning permission and building regulations

Most Birmingham homeowners **don't need planning permission** to re-roof, but there are exceptions such as listed buildings, flats and conservation areas. **Building regulations** usually do apply to a full re-roof.
