## Common roof repairs we can get you quotes for

- **Slipped, cracked or missing tiles and slates**, often after high winds
- **Roof leaks**: tracing where water is getting in, which is often not directly above the damp patch
- **Ridge and hip tiles**: re-bedding loose ridge tiles or switching to a dry-fix ridge system
- **Valleys**: the channels where two roof slopes meet, a common source of leaks
- **Lead flashing** around chimneys, walls and skylights
- **Storm damage** and temporary emergency repairs

| --- | --- |

| Find and fix a leak | £150 – £600 |
| Re-point or re-bed ridge tiles (about 10m) | £300 – £700 |
| Dry-fix ridge system (semi-detached) | £500 – £1,600 |
| Repair lead flashing | £200 – £600 |
| Patch repair to a flat roof | £150 – £800 |
| Scaffolding (two-storey house, if needed) | £500 – £1,200 |

These are typical ranges from 2026 UK price guides, including VAT. Small jobs can sometimes be done from a ladder or roof ladder, which avoids the cost of scaffolding.

## Signs your roof needs repairing

- Damp patches or brown stains on bedroom ceilings
- Tiles or pieces of tile on the ground or in the gutter
- Daylight visible in the loft, or damp felt and timbers
- Ridge tiles that look uneven or have gaps in the mortar
- Dripping or water marks around the chimney breast

Spot any of these? It's worth acting quickly. A small leak left over winter can soak insulation, rot timbers and damage plaster, turning a £300 repair into a much bigger bill.
