## Typical roof repair prices (2026)

| Repair | Typical cost |
| --- | --- |
| Replace one tile or slate | £80 – £200 |
| Replace a few slipped or cracked tiles | £150 – £500 |
| Trace a leak | £80 – £250 + repair |
| Re-point or re-bed ridge tiles (about 10m) | £300 – £700 |
| Dry-fix ridge system (semi-detached) | £500 – £1,600 |
| Repair lead flashing | £200 – £600 |
| Patch repair to a flat roof | £150 – £800 |
| Scaffolding on a two-storey house | £500 – £1,200 |

Prices are typical 2026 UK ranges and usually include VAT, but check any written quote. Birmingham prices generally sit within these ranges.

## The biggest factor: access

On many small repairs, getting up to the roof safely costs more than the repair itself. A roofer may be able to use a ladder and roof ladder on a bungalow or a low section. A two- or three-storey roof, or a steep one, usually needs a scaffold tower or full scaffolding.

**Tip:** if you need several jobs doing, such as a few tiles, a gutter and some flashing, get them done in one visit while the access is in place.

## When a repair stops making sense

Repairs are good value when the rest of the roof is sound. They're less good value when:

- You're calling a roofer out every year for a different leak
- The tiles are crumbling or the nails holding them have rusted through ("nail fatigue")
- The felt underneath has perished
- The roof is sagging

As a rule of thumb, if your roof needs its third repair in five years, it's worth getting a replacement price too. See [repair or replace?](/guides/repair-or-replace-roof/)
