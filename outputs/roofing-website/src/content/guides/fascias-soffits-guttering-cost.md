## Typical roofline costs (2026)

| Job | Typical cost |
| --- | --- |
| uPVC fascias, soffits and gutters, 3-bed semi | £1,800 – £3,500 |
| uPVC fascias, soffits and gutters, detached | £3,000 – £6,000 |
| Timber fascias, semi-detached | £1,800 – £3,500 |
| uPVC guttering only, per metre | £25 – £45 |
| Aluminium or cast iron guttering, per metre | £60 – £120 |

Figures are typical 2026 UK ranges from several price guides, which vary quite a lot. Birmingham prices generally sit within them.

## What affects the price

- **Scaffolding:** the biggest variable. It's sometimes included and sometimes listed separately.
- **House size and number of sides:** a terrace has two short runs; a detached house has four longer ones.
- **Material:** uPVC is cheapest; timber, aluminium and cast iron cost more.
- **Removal method:** replacing the old boards completely costs more than "capping" them (covering old timber with uPVC). Capping is cheaper but can hide rot, so ask which you're getting.
- **Extras:** dry verge systems, bird guards and ventilation are often added at the same time.

## Signs it's time to replace them

- Gutters overflowing, sagging or leaking at the joints
- Flaking paint and soft, rotten timber
- Damp patches or green streaks on outside walls
- Birds, wasps or squirrels getting in behind the boards

## Chimneys while the scaffolding is up

If you have a chimney, ask the roofer to check the pointing and lead flashing at the same time. Chimney repointing typically costs £400 to £1,200, and doing it while the access is already there can save a separate scaffolding bill.
