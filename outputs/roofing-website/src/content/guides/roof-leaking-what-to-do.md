## Do these things now

1. **Catch the water.** Put a bucket, bin or washing-up bowl under the drip. Put a towel in it to stop splashing.
2. **Stay safe around electrics.** If water is coming through near a light fitting or sockets, **switch off the electricity at the consumer unit** (fuse box). Don't touch wet fittings.
3. **Protect your things.** Move furniture, electronics and anything valuable away. Cover what you can't move with plastic sheeting or bin bags.
4. **Watch the ceiling.** If the plaster is bulging, keep people and pets out of the room. Don't poke it.
5. **Take photos and video** of the damage inside and anything you can see outside from the ground, such as missing tiles. Your insurer will want them.
6. **Check the loft** if it's safe and easy to get into. Put a container under any drips and move stored items away.
7. **Call a roofer.** [Tell us it's an emergency](/get-a-quote/?job=repair) or call or text us, and we'll flag it as urgent.

## Don't do these

- **Don't go on the roof.** Not in the rain, not with a ladder, not "just for a look".
- **Don't ignore it.** A slow leak soaks insulation, rots timber and spreads damp, and the repair bill grows quickly.
- **Don't pay a cold caller.** People knocking after storms offering "urgent" roof work are a well-known scam. Use a roofer you've checked.

## Common causes of roof leaks

| Cause | What to look for |
| --- | --- |
| Slipped or broken tiles | Gaps in the roof, tile pieces on the ground |
| Failed lead flashing | Damp around the chimney breast or where the roof meets a wall |
| Blocked gutters or valleys | Overflowing gutters, damp at the top of walls |
| Worn ridge tiles | Loose or missing ridge tiles, cracked mortar |
| Old or damaged flat roof | Puddles, blisters, damp below an extension |
| Condensation (not a leak) | Damp loft timbers and felt, often in winter |

## What happens next

A roofer will usually make the roof watertight first, sometimes with a temporary repair, and then quote for a permanent fix. Typical repair prices are in our [roof repair cost guide](/guides/roof-repair-cost/).
