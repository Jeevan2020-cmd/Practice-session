## New roof prices by house type (2026)

| House type and material | Typical cost |
| --- | --- |
| Mid-terrace, 2 to 3 bed, concrete tiles | £4,500 – £8,000 |
| Semi-detached, 3 bed, concrete tiles | £6,000 – £11,500 |
| Semi-detached, 3 bed, clay tiles | £9,500 – £15,500 |
| Semi-detached, 3 bed, natural slate | £11,000 – £18,500 |
| Detached | Priced on roof area and complexity: get a survey |

Figures are typical 2026 UK ranges, which Birmingham prices generally sit within. They usually include stripping the old roof, new battens, breathable membrane and new tiles. Check whether scaffolding and skip hire are included.

## What pushes the price up or down

1. **Roof size and shape.** Hips, valleys, dormers and chimneys all add labour and lead work. A simple gable roof on a terrace is the cheapest to replace.
2. **Material.** Concrete tiles are the budget option. Clay tiles and natural slate cost more but last longer and suit period homes.
3. **Scaffolding.** Needed on almost every re-roof. Height, access and how long it's up all affect the cost.
4. **Hidden timber problems.** Rotten rafters or boards only show up once the old roof comes off. Good quotes give a price per item for this, so there are no surprises.
5. **Insulation.** If building regulations apply, you may need to add loft or rafter insulation.
6. **Extras.** New fascias and gutters, dry-fix ridge systems and roof lights are often done at the same time, since the scaffolding is already up.

## How to compare roofing quotes

When you get a quote, check it covers:

- Scaffolding and skip hire
- Stripping and disposal of the old roof
- Membrane, battens, tiles and fixings (brand and type)
- Ridge, hips and valleys: dry-fix or mortar?
- Lead flashing
- What happens if rotten timber is found, and at what price
- Building regulations sign-off
- The written guarantee and how long it lasts

The cheapest quote isn't always the best value. A roof that leaks after two years costs far more than one done properly.
