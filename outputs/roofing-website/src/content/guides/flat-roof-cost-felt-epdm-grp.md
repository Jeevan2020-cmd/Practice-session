## Flat roof materials side by side

| | Felt | EPDM rubber | GRP fibreglass |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Typical cost per m² | £60 – £100 | £80 – £120 | £100 – £160 |
| Typical lifespan | 10 – 20 years | 20 – 30 years | 25 – 40 years |
| Single garage (15–20m²) | £1,200 – £2,000 | £1,500 – £2,400 | £1,800 – £3,000 |
| Finish | Mineral or smooth felt | Black rubber sheet | Hard, smooth resin coat |
| Best for | Budget garages | Extensions, garages | Walk-on roofs, complex shapes |

Prices are supply and fit from 2026 UK guides and generally exclude scaffolding. Budget roughly £400 to £900 for access on a garage, if needed.

## Extra costs to watch for

- **Rotten deck:** if the boards under the old covering are soft, they need replacing. Typically £15 to £35 per m².
- **Insulation ("warm roof"):** often required by building regulations when most of the roof is replaced. Typically £30 to £60 per m².
- **Edges and upstands:** trims, drip edges and where the roof meets a wall all need finishing properly.
- **Gutters:** a good time to replace them if they're tired.

A quote that only prices the top layer may not have allowed for any of these. Ask.

## Which should you choose?

- **Tight budget, simple garage roof:** modern multi-layer felt.
- **Extension or garage you want to forget about:** EPDM rubber.
- **Roof terrace, balcony or awkward shape:** GRP fibreglass.

Whichever material you choose, the installer matters more than the brand. Ask for a written guarantee covering both the materials and the workmanship.
